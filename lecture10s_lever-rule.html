The lever rule: how much of each phase

In a two-phase field the liquid and solid have different compositions. The lever rule tells you how much of each is present.

The copper–nickel phase diagram: liquid on top, solid α on the bottom, a two-phase lens between.

Why it matters: the lever rule turns a phase diagram into a recipe — how much solid has formed at a given temperature during casting, and therefore how a part solidifies.
